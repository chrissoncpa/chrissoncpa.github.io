Finance systems thesis

Thesis

Accountants aren’t ledger keepers anymore. They’re business systems architects.

The future of accounting isn’t closing the books — it’s orchestrating the data. Own the definitions. Control the inputs. Enforce quality and standards. Then let automation take over to turn numbers into narrative.

Traditional accounting organizations still treat CPAs as ledger keepers. Most Salesforce architects don’t understand GAAP. I’m both — and I use AI to compound the leverage. That’s the seam where modern finance lives.

I’ve also spent years on the GTM side of the seam — building the ARR mechanics, capacity models, and intra-quarter forecasts that Sales, CS, and leadership run off of. Strategic finance lands harder when the person building the model knows where the actuals come from.

Case studies

  1. 01

    Modern accounting stack — usage-based revenue

    Controller · Newo.ai

    Designing the finance architecture for a Series A AI voice platform: contracts and usage metering flowing through a revenue recognition and AR layer under ASC 606, joining payables and payroll at a single general ledger of record. Recognition is built into the pipeline rather than reconciled after the fact.

    Why it matters · Usage-based revenue breaks the standard SaaS rev-rec playbook. The architecture has to meter, price, and recognize in one flow.
